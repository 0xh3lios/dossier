## Findings

**F1. A1 under all-honest is G3 and G4 restated for the stored pair (formal:2, formal:6).** Verified, by reading the generated module.

- G3 and G4 restrict each honest post to `value + 1 >= oracle.price and value <= oracle.price + 1` (prose.md lines 16-17; Upkeep.tla lines 71-72, 83-84).
- M4 and M5 then set last to the old price and price to the value (lines 75-76, 87-88).
- A tick changes neither price nor last (lines 92-95).

So under honest lanes A1 cannot fail. The cell confirms only that last records the price it replaces. It does not confirm any restraint applied by the Oracle, which has none (lines 68-90).

The claim states G3 and G4 as its assumptions. The explanation says the model restricts an honest lane's submitted value (behavior line 70). The claim is therefore not overstated.

The `jumpy` blunder confirms that A1 depends on those Givens. By reading the module, a copy that stopped updating last would also break A1, since two honest posts could carry the price two steps from a stale last. No answer records that.

**F2. The acceptance speaks to the trace (formal:15).** Verified.

**F3. The composition's G3 and G4 repeat the member's, and the module applies both copies (formal:6).** Verified. Minor.

The scenario's `stronger_than: [all-honest]` is sound: the only mode that differs is malicious, and the start is the same.
