- Statement: `modules/adapter/state/quantities.md` lines 5 to 21 record the Adapter's `held` as "Credit held by the adapter, read from the vault's issued slot", read from `vault#contract:vault` by `issued()`. The record behind the Adapter's K1 says to check it by reading `vault.issued` and `token.totalSupply` (`modules/adapter/behavior/invariants.md` line 16). In the composed module, `adapter_held` and `vault_issued` are two variables, each changed by its own member's move in the same wired step (`compositions/supply/model/generated/Supply.tla` lines 124 and 127, 137 and 141, 150 and 154). So A3 compares with the supply a variable whose counterpart in the knowledge base is the quantity A2 already compares with it. In source terms A3 restates A2, and the model's consequence of A2 and A3, held equal to issued, is an identity there. The explanation says "Two equalities are needed. Issued credit must match the Token's total supply, and the Adapter's held balance must match that supply too." (`compositions/supply/behaviors/match-token-backing.md` line 127) without saying that this is the model's view. C3 and C4 are scoped to the model and stay supported.
- Affects: the explanation; the limits of C3 and C4; A3's meaning outside the model; the Adapter's K1.
- Status: verified as to the records and the module. Whether the deployed Adapter keeps a balance of its own is not verified, since the knowledge base holds no Adapter source (G5).
- Scope: correspondence between the model and the source. The model claims are unaffected.
- Missing evidence: the Adapter's pinned source, or a record that says whether held is stored apart from issued.

- Affects: cells A2 and A3 under user-malicious, trials 2026-11-24-1432-tlc-bdef73 and 2026-11-24-1432-tlc-533869; claims C2 and C4.
