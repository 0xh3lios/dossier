- Affects: A2, E5, M5, M6 and K4; claim C2 and the explanation.

- Status: verified, for the omission in the prose and the silence in the behavior.
- Scope: the abstraction and this behavior's text. The mapping is scoped to the model, so it stays supported, with this limit attached.
- Not verified: whether A2 survives the lowering path. That depends on the guard the owner states.
