### F1. The explanation's "does not trap" is broader than the claims that map it

- Statement: "Entry remains available while its amount is covered by held credit, so the halt does not trap already issued HRD in this model" (`return-after-halt.md` line 94) is a statement about every Halted state. The claims map one path (C2, W2 at amount 1) and two properties of Halted (C1, A1; C3, R1). In the record, the general statement rests on M3's chart check, `M3: Halted receives enter as said` (`chart-64fcc7.json` line 101), with R2 (line 125) and the check that at most one move or refusal applies (line 160). So at every explored Halted snapshot an entry of at most held is accepted, and held can be stepped to 0 from any Halted snapshot within the chart's ranges. No claim maps M3, and the behavior report in `dossier verify --json` lists M3 and M4 among the passages no behavior is associated with. The same holds for "A Breaker halt moves the Adapter from Open to Halted and clears live" (line 94), which is M4 (`prose.md` line 21) and is carried by no claim. C1 gives only its consequence, A1. "Already issued HRD" also reads held credit as outstanding HRD, which the local model does not hold, since it has no token supply. S2's text, "entries continue so HRD can always return" (`prose.md` line 10), makes the same possibility statement, which S2's own check, reachability (`chart-64fcc7.json` line 53), does not establish.
- Affected: the explanation; passages M3, M4, S2.
- Status: verified.
- Missing: a claim mapped to M3 and R2, and one mapped to M4, if the keeper wants these general statements carried.

### F2. A1 relates Halted and live in one direction, and the knowledge record gates exit by live

A source or deployment reading of this behavior would need the converse and the correspondence between the source's live flag and the Halted state, and no claim pins that record.
- Affected: A1; C1 if it were widened beyond the model.
- Status: verified.
- Missing: for any claim beyond the model, a pinned record and a correspondence between the source's live flag and the Halted state.
